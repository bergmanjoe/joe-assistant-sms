What this service is

Joseph Bergman Personal Assistant is the AI phone assistant (named Nico) for Joe Bergman Photography, a sole proprietorship of Joseph Bergman. It answers calls and sends business text messages from +1 (661) 531-1244 to two groups only:

  • The owner, Joseph Bergman: account, sign-in and approval alerts, plus follow-ups on calls the assistant handled.
  • Team members who help with the business and have opted in themselves by texting YES or START to the assistant's number: business messages Joe asks the assistant to pass along (shoot schedules, client updates, pickups and logistics for jobs) and urgent business heads-ups.

It never texts the public or anyone who hasn't signed up, and it sends no marketing.

How recipients opt in

Joseph Bergman opted in his own mobile number on October 21, 2026 by entering it as an alert recipient in the assistant's configuration. A team member opts in only by texting YES or START to +1 (661) 531-1244 from their own phone, sending only the single word YES (or START) as the whole message. That text is the opt-in and the consent record. Joe never signs anyone up on their behalf, and a verbal agreement alone is not enough to add a number. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or employment. The sender immediately receives this confirmation, and nothing else is sent before it:

"Joseph Bergman Personal Assistant: You're signed up for texts from Joe's assistant. Up to 10 msgs/day. Msg&data rates may apply. HELP for help, STOP to end."

Sign-up instructions are posted on the home page and in the SMS Terms.

Information collected

  • The owner's mobile number, entered by him. For team members, only the mobile number their opt-in text was sent from; no other personal information is collected from them.
  • The opt-in text itself (YES or START, with its date and time), kept as the consent record.
  • Message content: alerts to the owner (sign-ins, approvals, call follow-ups), business messages the owner asks the assistant to pass along to team members, and urgent business heads-ups.
  • Delivery status records (sent, delivered, failed) and STOP/HELP keyword replies.
  • Basic details of phone calls to the assistant number, used to answer calls. Call recording is off, and no transcripts are kept.

How information is used

Only to deliver these messages and to troubleshoot delivery.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information is processed only by the service providers needed to deliver messages and calls, under their own terms.

Opt-out and help

Any recipient can reply STOP at any time to stop all texts. Reply HELP for help, or email mail@jbphotoandco.com. Msg & data rates may apply. Message frequency varies, up to 10 messages per day per recipient. Messages contain no links.

Retention

Message and delivery records are kept up to 90 days. Call records keep only basic details, with no recordings or transcripts. Opt-in consent records (the YES or START text and its date and time) are kept for as long as the person is signed up and for one year after they opt out.

Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date. See also the SMS Terms.
