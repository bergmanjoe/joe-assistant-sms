What this service is

A personal AI phone assistant operated by Joseph Bergman for his own use. It sends text messages from +1 (661) 531-1244 to exactly one recipient: the account owner's own mobile phone. It does not text the public, customers, or any third party.

Information collected

  • The account owner's own mobile phone number, entered by the owner as the single alert recipient.
  • Message content generated by the assistant: alerts about sign-ins, approvals, missed calls, and similar items needing the owner's attention.
  • Delivery status records (sent, delivered, failed) and STOP/HELP keyword replies.
  • Basic details of phone calls to the assistant number, used to answer calls. Call recording is off, and no transcripts are kept.

How information is used

Only to deliver alerts to the account owner and to troubleshoot delivery.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information is processed only by the service providers needed to deliver messages and calls, under their own terms.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts. Reply HELP for help, or email mail@jbphotoandco.com. Msg & data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Retention

Alert and delivery records are kept up to 90 days. Call records keep only basic details, with no recordings or transcripts.

Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date. See also the SMS Terms.
